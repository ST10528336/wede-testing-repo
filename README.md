## Project Overview

This is a family business that was found in 2024 by Mpho Mofokeng, and this business focuses on making cleaning detergents and products that we use on our daily basis to clean our homes and workplaces. 

Uncle’s Wife Cleaning Detergents was established to provide affordable, high-quality cleaning detergents to households and businesses. 

Uncle’s Wife Cleaning Detergents focuses on delivering reliable products and excellent customer service. 

The long-term goal is to expand into surrounding communities, supplying local businesses and become the trusted brand in the cleaning industry. 

---

## Website Goals and adjectives

### Goals
The goal in creating the website is so that we can increase sales by allowing customers to place orders or make enquiries and showcase our cleaning detergents products with descriptions or the products and prices. 

Allowing customers to contact the business through a contact form, phone number, WhatsApp, or email and including all the information about the company which will be the history, mission and vision of the business. 

To promote the business and build brand awareness and provide different customer support by sharing the contact information and answering questions asked by customers.  

### KPIs
Increase the number of website visitors to at least 300 new visitors per months within the first four months. 

Get at least 45 online or WhatsApp enquiries per month through the website. 

Increase the number of orders by at least 5% per month within the first six months of releasing the new website (Chaffey & Ellis-Chadwick, 2022). 

Increase the customer satisfaction rate of at least 80% based on customers experience and feedback/reviews 

Ensure the website has an uptime that is high so that visitors can access it whenever needed.

## Part 3 Details

### JavaScript Functionality

JavaScript was added to improve the website's functionality and user experience.

The following features were implemented:

- Added a message to the Learn More button on the Home page.
- Added automatic updating of the footer year.
- Added validation to the Enquiries form to check that the required fields are completed.
- Added validation to the Contact Us form to check the visitor's name, email address, message type and message.
- Added a loading message while the Contact Us form is being submitted.
- Added success and error messages to provide feedback to visitors.
- Added a product search feature that allows visitors to search for products by name or description.
- Added a service search feature that allows visitors to search for services.

### Contact Us Form and AJAX

The Contact Us form was connected to Formspree using the JavaScript Fetch API. This allows form information to be submitted asynchronously without opening Outlook or reloading the website.

The form collects the visitor's name, email address, message type and message. After a successful submission, a confirmation message is displayed and the form fields are cleared.

The form was tested, and the submitted information appeared in the Formspree dashboard, including the sender's details and message.

### Enquiries Form

The Enquiries form includes HTML form validation and JavaScript validation. It checks that the required fields are completed and displays a response message after submission.

The current Enquiries form displays a success message but has not yet been connected to Formspree. Therefore, the message is not confirmed as delivered to the business.

### Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)

On-page SEO improvements were made to the website.

The improvements included:

- Adding page-specific titles.
- Adding meta descriptions to the HTML pages.
- Using headings to organise website content.
- Adding descriptive alternative text to images.
- Maintaining internal navigation links between website pages.
- Creating a `robots.txt` file to provide instructions to search-engine crawlers.
- Creating a `sitemap.xml` file to describe the website's page structure.

The sitemap must be updated with the final published website URLs before deployment is complete.

### Contact Page Improvements

The Contact Us page was updated to improve its layout and usability.

- Contact information is displayed on the left side.
- A Google Maps embed showing Springs Mall is positioned directly underneath the location details.
- The contact form is displayed on the right side on larger screens.
- The layout changes to one column on smaller screens.

The Springs Mall map is used as a location reference and should not be understood as the business's actual premises unless confirmed.
