## Project Title: Uncle's Wife Cleaning Detergents

### Description of the Project

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate the web development skills I have learned by creating a simple, professional and responsive website for Uncle's Wife Cleaning Detergents.

The website promotes the business, displays its cleaning products and services, and allows customers to find business information and submit enquiries. It was developed as part of the Web Development (Introduction) module.

## Project Overview

Uncle's Wife Cleaning Detergents is a family business founded in 2024 by Mpho Mofokeng. The business focuses on providing cleaning detergents and products for everyday use in homes and workplaces.

The business was established to provide affordable, high-quality cleaning detergents to households and businesses.

Uncle's Wife Cleaning Detergents focuses on delivering reliable products and excellent customer service.

The long-term goal is to expand into surrounding communities, supply local businesses and become a trusted brand in the cleaning industry.

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Goals

- Increase sales by allowing customers to make product enquiries and learn about available products.
- Showcase cleaning detergents with descriptions, available sizes and prices.
- Allow customers to contact the business through the contact form, phone, WhatsApp and email.
- Provide information about the company's history, mission and vision.
- Promote the business and build brand awareness.
- Improve customer support by providing business information and responding to customer enquiries.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Increase website traffic to at least 300 new visitors per month within the first four months.
- Receive at least 45 online or WhatsApp enquiries per month through the website.
- Increase the number of orders by at least 5% per month within the first six months of launching the website (Chaffey and Ellis-Chadwick, 2022).
- Achieve a customer satisfaction rate of at least 80% based on customer feedback and reviews.
- Maintain high website availability so that visitors can access the website when needed.

## Part 1 Details

### Target Audience

The target audience includes households, families and individuals who need cleaning products for everyday use.

The business also targets schools, offices, cleaning companies and other organisations that require affordable and reliable cleaning detergents.

Healthcare facilities and daycare centres are also potential customers because they require high standards of cleanliness.

### Design and User Experience

#### 1. Colour Scheme

The website uses the following colours:

- **Deep Blue (#1E88E5):** Represents cleanliness and professionalism and helps create a trustworthy appearance.
- **White (#FFFFFF):** Gives the website a clean and professional appearance while improving readability.
- **Light Grey (#F5F5F5):** Provides a simple background that allows the products and other content to stand out.

#### 2. Typography

- **Headings – Poppins:** Used to make headings stand out and improve the overall appearance of the website.
- **Body Text – Open Sans:** Used for paragraphs, product descriptions and other website content to provide a comfortable reading experience.

#### 3. Layout and Design Approach

The website uses a clean and professional design that reflects the business's focus on quality and cleanliness.

The layout aims to provide a trustworthy and user-friendly experience, making it easy for customers to navigate the website and find the information they need.

## Part 3 Details

### JavaScript Functionality

JavaScript was added to improve the website's functionality and user experience.

The following features were implemented:

- Added a message to the Learn More button on the Home page.
- Added automatic updating of the footer year.
- Added validation to the Enquiries form.
- Added validation to the Contact Us form.
- Added loading, success and error messages for form submissions.
- Added a product search feature that allows visitors to search for products by name or description.
- Added a service search feature that allows visitors to search for services.

### Contact Us Form and AJAX

The Contact Us form was connected to Formspree using the JavaScript Fetch API.

This allows form information to be submitted asynchronously without opening Outlook or reloading the website.

The form collects the visitor's name, email address, message type and message.

After a successful submission, a confirmation message is displayed and the form fields are cleared.

The form was tested successfully, and the submitted information appeared in the Formspree dashboard.

### Enquiries Form

The Enquiries form allows customers to submit questions about the business, products and services.

The form includes the following fields:

- Full Name
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Enquiry Type
- Enquiry Message

HTML form validation checks that the required fields are completed and that the information follows the specified input requirements.

The Enquiries form was connected to Formspree and tested successfully. A confirmation message is displayed after submission, and the submitted enquiry can be viewed in the Formspree dashboard.
