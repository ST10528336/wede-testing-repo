About Us

Brief History

This is a family business that was found in 2024 by Mpho Mofokeng, and this business focuses on making cleaning detergents and products that we use on our daily basis to clean our homes and workplaces.

Uncle's Wife Cleaning Detergents was established to provide affordable, high-quality cleaning detergents to households and businesses.

Uncle's Wife Cleaning Detergents focuses on delivering reliable products and excellent customer service.

Mission

The mission for the business is to sell affordable, high-quality cleaning detergents like dishwashing soap and bleach to the community around us so that people won't travel far distance to have access to these detergents.

Vision

The vision is to see the business growing to be able to create more job opportunities around the community allowing people to experience and see what the key to a safe and useable detergents for their health and households is.
